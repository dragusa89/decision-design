The Diagnostic

Most people know something is off.
Few can name where.

The Diagnostic is a structured conversation that maps where decision control is being lost in your work — which conversations are happening without you, and what frame is being set before you arrive.

What it is

One session, 60 to 90 minutes.

We work through your actual situation — a specific client, a specific stakeholder, a specific negotiation.

How it works
01

You apply

A short description of what is going on. Two paragraphs is enough.

02

I write back

If it is a fit, I will reach out with dates. If it is not, I will say so directly.

03

We talk

One session, based on what you sent. You get the clarity document.

What you get

A Clarity document.

After the session you receive a written overview of your situation. It covers what came out of the conversation, what I observed, where the leverage sits, and what changes if you act on it.

It is yours to keep and use, whether or not we work together again.

Apply

Tell me what is going on.

I read every application and write back.
